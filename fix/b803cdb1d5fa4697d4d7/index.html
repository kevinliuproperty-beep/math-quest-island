Fix-up 1 · Time
A ferry left at 11:40 and arrived at 16:20. How long was the journey?
You wrote: 4 h 20 min. So close! The right answer is a little more.
TrapDon't take away times like normal numbers. An hour has 60 minutes, not 100. Count up to the next whole hour instead.
  1. From 11:40, count up to the next whole hour: 11:40 → 12:00 is 20 min.
  2. Jump whole hours: 12:00 → 16:00 is 4 h.
  3. The last bit: 16:00 → 16:20 is 20 min.
  4. Add them up: 20 min + 4 h + 20 min = 4 h 40 min.

Try one like it

A bus left at 10:35 and arrived at 14:15. How long was the journey?

10:35 → 11:00 is 25 min.

11:00 → 14:00 is 3 h.

14:00 → 14:15 is 15 min.

25 min + 3 h + 15 min = 3 h 40 min.

Taking 1035 from 1415 like normal numbers gives 380, which would wrongly look like "3 h 80 min".

Fix-up 2 · Posts and gaps
Posts stand equally far apart along a fence. The 1st post is 10 m from the 3rd post. The 3rd post is 45 m from the last post. How many posts are there altogether?
You wrote: 9. You found something true, but it was the number of gaps in one part, not the number of posts.
TrapCount the gaps first. Metres measure gaps. Then posts = gaps + 1.
  1. From the 1st post to the 3rd post there are 2 gaps (not 3!). They make 10 m.
  2. So one gap is 10 ÷ 2 = 5 m.
  3. From the 3rd post to the last: 45 ÷ 5 = 9 gaps. (This is where 9 came from.)
  4. All the gaps: 2 + 9 = 11 gaps. Posts = gaps + 1 = 12 posts.

Try one like it

Trees are planted equally far apart along a path. The 1st tree is 12 m from the 4th tree. The 4th tree is 36 m from the last tree. How many trees are there altogether?

1st → 4th tree is 3 gaps. One gap = 12 ÷ 3 = 4 m.

4th → last tree: 36 ÷ 4 = 9 gaps.

All the gaps: 3 + 9 = 12 gaps. Trees = 12 + 1 = 13 trees.

Fix-up 3 · Bar graph
The bar graph shows the number of cups of sugarcane juice sold at the stall from Monday to Friday. Each cup was sold for $3. How much more money did the stall collect on Friday than on Thursday?
You wrote: $30. The answer is much bigger. Let's read the graph slowly.
TrapRead the scale first. Find the difference in cups, then × $3 to turn cups into money.

This graph is like the one in your paper. The bars may not be exactly the same, but the steps are.

  1. Read the scale: the numbers go up in 20s, so each line is 10 cups. Don't just count lines!
  2. Thursday: 50 cups. Friday: 120 cups.
  3. How many more cups on Friday? 120 − 50 = 70 cups.
  4. The question asks for money: 70 × $3 = $210.

Try one like it

A stall sold 40 cups of soya milk on Tuesday and 90 cups on Wednesday. Each cup was sold for $4. How much more money did the stall collect on Wednesday than on Tuesday?

More cups on Wednesday: 90 − 40 = 50 cups.

Money: 50 × $4 = $200.

Fix-up 4 · Bar model
Jun Hao had twice as many beads as Aisyah. After Jun Hao gave 382 beads to Aisyah, they had the same number of beads. How many beads did Aisyah have at first?
You left this one blank. It was a tricky one, so let's crack it together.
TrapDraw the bar model. To make two people equal, the bigger one gives away half the difference.
  1. Draw it: Jun Hao has 2 units, Aisyah has 1 unit. The difference is 1 unit.
  2. To end up equal, Jun Hao gives away half of that extra unit. So half a unit = 382.
  3. One whole unit = 382 × 2 = 764.
  4. Aisyah had 1 unit at first = 764 beads. Check: Jun Hao 1528 − 382 = 1146 and Aisyah 764 + 382 = 1146. Same! ✓

Try one like it

Mei had twice as many stickers as Lina. After Mei gave 145 stickers to Lina, they had the same number of stickers. How many stickers did Lina have at first?

Mei 2 units, Lina 1 unit. Mei gives half the difference, so half a unit = 145.

1 unit = 145 × 2 = 290. Lina had 290 stickers at first.

Check: Mei 580 − 145 = 435 and Lina 290 + 145 = 435. Same! ✓
