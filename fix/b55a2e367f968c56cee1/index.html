Give and Compare

Five bar-model questions about giving. Start with the big idea below. For each question, draw the bars first, then type your answer and press Check. Stuck? Press Next step any time to see the bar model one bit at a time.

The big idea
When one person gives some to another, the gap between them changes by double the amount.

Why double? The giver loses it, and the receiver gains it. That's two changes, not one.

RememberDraw the bars before any sums. Count units carefully: "twice as many" means 2 units, not 1.
Question 1 · Warm-up
Mei and Ravi had the same number of marbles. Mei gave 136 marbles to Ravi. How many more marbles does Ravi have than Mei now?
  1. Draw two bars the same length.
  2. Mei gives away 136, so Mei's bar gets 136 shorter.
  3. Ravi gets those 136, so Ravi's bar gets 136 longer.
  4. The gap is 136 + 136 = 272. Ravi has 272 more marbles. (Not 136!)
Question 2
Arjun had 75 more stamps than Siti. Then Siti gave 120 stamps to Arjun. How many more stamps does Arjun have than Siti now?
  1. Draw it: Arjun's bar is 75 longer than Siti's at the start.
  2. Siti gives 120, so Siti's bar gets 120 shorter.
  3. Arjun gets 120, so Arjun's bar gets 120 longer.
  4. Measure the new gap: 120 + 75 + 120 = 315. Arjun has 315 more stamps.
Question 3
Hui Min has 248 more beads than Ravi. How many beads must Hui Min give to Ravi so that they both have the same number?
  1. Draw it: Hui Min's bar is 248 longer than Ravi's.
  2. Cut the extra 248 into two halves.
  3. Hui Min gives one half to Ravi. She loses a half and he gains a half, so the whole gap closes.
  4. 248 ÷ 2 = 124. Hui Min gives 124 beads. (Giving all 248 would make Ravi 248 ahead!)
Question 4
Siti had twice as many shells as Mei. After Siti gave 217 shells to Mei, they had the same number of shells. How many shells did Mei have at first?
  1. Draw it: Siti has 2 units, Mei has 1 unit. The difference is 1 unit.
  2. To end up the same, Siti gives half the difference. So half a unit = 217.
  3. One whole unit = 217 × 2 = 434.
  4. Mei had 1 unit at first = 434 shells. Check: Siti 868 − 217 = 651 and Mei 434 + 217 = 651. Same! ✓
Question 5 · Challenge
Arjun has twice as many cards as Ravi. Hui Min has 158 more cards than Ravi. They have 838 cards altogether. How many cards does Arjun have?
  1. Draw it: Ravi is 1 unit. Arjun has twice as many, so Arjun is 2 units. Hui Min is 1 unit + 158.
  2. Count every unit: 2 + 1 + 1 = 4 units (not 3!). Take away the extra 158: 838 − 158 = 680.
  3. 4 units = 680, so 1 unit = 680 ÷ 4 = 170. That's Ravi.
  4. Arjun is 2 units = 170 × 2 = 340 cards. Check: 340 + 170 + 328 = 838 ✓
